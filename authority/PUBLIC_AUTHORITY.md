# Public authority boundary

The public repository records, but does not re-upload, the exact GNU-source
archive used as authority for the Indonesian edition:

- 2,125,284 bytes
- SHA-256
  `d1adaae8f205ac53113b902267ca3415422500b6ff92b1137fa11c7c8fbb26e9`
- official upstream download:
  `https://math.dartmouth.edu/~prob/prob/prob.tar.gz`

The archive is omitted from the corrected public tree as a conservative
component-rights measure: it contains historical third-party plates that the
Indonesian edition has independently redrawn. The size and SHA-256 above allow
the authority byte stream to be recovered and verified from its official
location without republishing those plates here.

The local authority directory preserves that frozen archive and other research inputs that are
**not** admitted to the public repository:

- the separately typeset AMS-layout PDF, because it is not the licensing
  authority for the GNU source;
- the 2008 odd-numbered-answer PDF and every translation/transcription derived
  from it, because no express derivative/republication license was found;
- other local extraction and inspection artifacts.

This boundary is intentional. It prevents the main book's GNU FDL notice from
being falsely applied to separately distributed material.
